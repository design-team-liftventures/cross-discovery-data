Method. PMF score = respondents answering 5 (“very disappointed”) ÷ all respondents to that question. Top-2 box adds
the 4s (“somewhat disappointed”). The 40% line is the Sean Ellis threshold commonly read as evidence of product-market
fit.
Grouping. The two Author Insights surveys (subscribers and visitors) are pooled into one surface, as are all Book Brief
responses. Games are reported separately because each ran its own survey.
Read with care. Reviews (n=8) and Book Brief (n=23) carry wide confidence intervals — treat their positions as
directional. Four responses carry a 1 September timestamp; they belong to the August wave and are counted in it.
