Part 1 — The full list
Requests fall into three groups. Content requests are what readers want us to publish or deepen; feature
requests are product capabilities; commercial requests concern how we sell. The third group is included
because it appears in every source and often as the stated condition for staying.

Catalog gaps are both the #1 request and a direct cause of avoidable early churn: several refund requests

• Users explicitly ask for the mechanism (“wish list”, “a recommendation feature for Guides we would like”).

Method and caveats
